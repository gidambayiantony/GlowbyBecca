

# Fitness Fanatics 254 — E-Commerce Website

## Brand Identity
- **Name**: Fitness Fanatics 254
- **Vibe**: Dark & bold — black/charcoal backgrounds with neon accent colors (electric green or vibrant orange), strong typography, high-energy feel
- **Tagline area**: Space for a motivational tagline on the hero section

---

## Page 1: Home Page
- **Hero Section**: Full-width banner with bold text overlay ("Gear Up. Show Up. Level Up." or similar), background image, and a prominent "Shop Now" CTA button
- **Featured Products**: Grid of 4-6 highlighted products with images, names, and "Order via WhatsApp" button on each
- **Gallery/Lookbook Section**: Instagram-style image grid showcasing fitness fits in action — clean, visual, aesthetic
- **Why Choose Us**: Brief section with icons (quality, fast delivery, style)
- **Instagram Feed CTA**: Link to the Instagram profile with a "Follow Us" call to action
- **WhatsApp Floating Button**: Always visible sticky WhatsApp button for quick ordering

## Page 2: Shop / Products Page
- **Category Filters**: Tabs or filter buttons for Gym Wear, Athleisure, and Accessories
- **Product Grid**: Clean card layout with product images, names, and "Inquire on WhatsApp" button (no prices displayed)
- **Quick View**: Click on a product to see more images and details in a modal/dialog
- **Each product card links to WhatsApp** with a pre-filled message like "Hi, I'm interested in [Product Name]"

## Page 3: Contact Page
- **Contact Info**: Phone number (+254706777368), WhatsApp link, Instagram link
- **WhatsApp CTA**: Large prominent "Chat with Us on WhatsApp" button
- **Location/Delivery Info**: Section for delivery areas or shipping info if applicable
- **Simple Contact Form**: Name, email, message (optional, for general inquiries)

## Global Elements
- **Navigation Bar**: Sleek dark navbar with logo/brand name, links to Home, Shop, Contact, and Instagram icon
- **Footer**: Brand name, social media links (Instagram), WhatsApp contact, copyright
- **Floating WhatsApp Button**: Persistent on all pages for easy ordering
- **Mobile-First Design**: Fully responsive, optimized for mobile shoppers
- **Smooth Animations**: Subtle fade-in and hover effects for a premium feel

## Product Data
- Products will be managed as static data in the code initially (no backend needed)
- Product images will use the photos you upload from Instagram
- Easy to add/update products later

## Tech Approach
- Frontend-only website (no backend or database needed)
- WhatsApp integration via direct links (wa.me/254706777368)
- Beautiful, fast, and lightweight
